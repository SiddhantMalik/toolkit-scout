Only Admin users can create API tokens. To learn more about Admins, see Roles User Management . Some common use cases for our API include: Fetching information about customers Fetching information about issues Creating issues programmatically Check out our API Reference for specifications on all endpoints. To call the API from TypeScript, Python, or Go, use our SDKs , with pinned installation commands and upgrade guidance for each language. Prefer the terminal? Try our experimental Pylon CLI . It’s an early release and may change. Step 1: Setting up Internal Threads Internal threads are a dedicated threads in Slack to discuss and collaborate on new customer issues internally. All discussion in these threads will be synced back to Pylon! We recommend setting this up prior to connecting any other channels so they ll be ready to sync as soon as you pull data into Pylon. For Small Teams Make one public channel called #customer-alerts . For Larger Teams If you have multiple team members handling accounts, we recommend opening up one channel per person on your team that s responsible for handling account messages. For example, if you have three CSMs Alex, Sabrina, and John: #alex-customers #sabrina-customers #john-customers If you’re in a public workspace, or just want to make your internal threads in a private channel, add Pylon to the channel following the below instructions, but choose Triage Channel . Go to Settings Internal Threads (under Workflows) and select the first template. Under Send To , pick the channel(s) you created above! You can always change where internal threads are created, or segment issues by account owner or other properties to create internal threads in different places for more complex team structures. Step 2: Enabling Channels Channels in Pylon are bucketed into three categories: Customer Channels, where you re talking directly with your customers Community Channels, which typically contain users from many different companies Internal Channels, where users within your company are asking questions to other team members Each type of channel can be enabled in a slightly different way. Enable a Customer Channel Pylon can be enabled in customer channels by one of 3 methods: Bulk updating via the Accounts dashboard in Pylon Manual enablement directly from a Slack channel Autolinking via standard naming conventions Private customer channels must be enabled directly in Slack. Accounts Dashboard Select Accounts in the sidebar, and then click the purple Add Account button in the top right of the screen (which should bring you to the menu below). Pick Slack to bulk enable Pylon in relevant customer channels. Slack Pylon will have function in customer channels that you explicitly enable it in. If you want it to be enabled in channels automatically, you can use our CRM widget to create and setup channels automatically. Go to your customer channel Type /invite @Pylon (or whatever you set as the Pylon bot display name ) and press enter. You’ll get a prompt to enable Pylon in the channel. Select Customer Channel . Fill out the modal with your preferences, including selecting the desired triage channel (these will be saved for the next time) Autolink Channels If you have a common pattern to your slack channels (i.e. putting ext- at the beginning of all external channels), you can autolink them with Pylon. Head over to the Account Configuration page and find the External Slack channel linking section. You ll be able to configure the pattern of your Slack channels there so they will sync automatically moving forward. Microsoft Teams has a separate automatic linking configuration for new standard and shared channels. See Microsoft Teams for eligibility and account-matching rules. Enable a Community Channel A community channel is a channel with users from many different companies, typically in a community workspace. Link this channel the same way you would a customer channel, but choose Community Channel once inviting Pylon to the channel. Enable an Internal Channel An internal channel is a channel where users from within your company are asking questions, typically to the support or customer-facing team. Pylon is a pretty powerful platform with lots of customization to make your day-to-day productive and fun. Here are some of the things you ll want to get setup or learn about to help you get the most value out of Pylon. Keyboard Shortcuts All keyboard shortcuts can be found in-app by choosing Keyboard Shortcuts from your profile menu at the bottom left corner of the screen. General Navigation To quickly navigate around Pylon, view recently visited pages, or search for specific customer issues, knowledge base articles, accounts, etc use Command Search by hitting Cmd-K or Ctrl-K from anywhere in Pylon. Managing Issues Issue keyboard shortcuts are available when you are viewing an issue. Shortcut Action j and k Navigate between issues s Change the status of an issue z Snooze an issue a Assign an issue f Toggle fullscreen mode m Run a Macro Esc Exit out of an issue Cmd-j AI Copilot Cmd-Enter or Ctrl-Enter Send a message Cmd-Shift-Enter or Ctrl-Shift-Enter Send a message and close the issue Cmd-Option-Enter or Ctrl-Option-Enter Send a message, close the issue, and move to the next issue Dark Mode Toggle dark mode from your Profile page in Settings or by typing Cmd-k and then Dark Mode . Notification Preferences Under the Notifications page, customize which notifications you d like to receive and where you d like to receive them. You can also access them from both the profile and settings menus in the bottom left corner of your screen. Notifications are grouped into tabs: Issues , Projects tasks , Knowledge base , Feature management , and Connectors tools . For each event, choose whether to be notified In-app , in Slack , with a Desktop notification, or by Email . Turn on Enable Sound to play a sound for in-app notifications. Issue notifications you can configure: I m assigned an issue by a teammate I m assigned an issue by triggers or assignment rules I m mentioned in an internal note New customer message on an issue I follow New message from your team on an issue I follow Issue that I follow is unsnoozed Issue that I m assigned to breached SLA Email notifications about issue messages include the message text, up to 5,000 characters. Longer messages are cut off with an ellipsis; open the link in the email to read the full message in Pylon. In-app notifications will show up under your notifications tray, accessible via. the Bell icon on the top left. The tray has All , Unread , and Mentions tabs, and a Mark all read button. If Desktop notifications are on but you aren t seeing them, check that your browser allows notifications from Pylon. Signatures If you ll be sending emails from Pylon, consider setting up an email signature on your Profile page.
