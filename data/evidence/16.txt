# LiveAgent > LiveAgent is help desk and call center software for small and growing support teams, in business since 2004: email, live chat, phone, social media and a knowledge base in one shared inbox, with AI that answers customers and drafts replies. Prices start at $15 per agent a month billed annually ($19 billed monthly) for email, live chat and knowledge base; call center and social channels start with the Team plan at $29 ($35). 30-day free trial, no credit card required. LiveAgent is also available in 19 other languages, each on its own domain: www.live-agent.cz, www.liveagent.dk, www.liveagent.de, www.liveagent.es, www.liveagent.fr, www.live-agent.it, www.liveagent.hu, www.live-agent.nl, www.live-agent.pl, www.liveagent.ro, www.liveagent.sk, www.live-agent.cn, www.liveagent.ph, www.liveagent.vn, www.liveagent.ae, www.liveagent.com.br, www.liveagent.jp, www.live-agent.se, www.live-agent.fi. Plans and prices, as the pricing page shows them: - Starter: $15 per agent/month billed annually, or $19 billed monthly. Per agent, up to 15 agents. Email ticketing and contact forms; Live chat and chatbot; Knowledge base; All AI features; Single sign-on (SSO). No social channels, call center or reporting. - Team: $29 per agent/month billed annually, or $35 billed monthly. Per agent, up to 30 agents. Everything in Starter; Facebook, Instagram, X, Viber, WhatsApp, Telegram; Call center; Reporting, CSAT surveys and time reporting; SLA rules and custom roles. - Business: $49 per agent/month billed annually, or $59 billed monthly. Per agent, any team size. Everything in Team; MCP server: connect your own AI tools to LiveAgent; LiveAgent branding removal; Audit log and department roles. - Enterprise: $3,199 per month billed annually, flat fee, or $3,999 billed monthly. 80 agents included. Everything in Business; Unlimited departments, channels, rules and roles; Dedicated account manager; Priority support and review calls; Custom billing and release management. These are the regular prices.
