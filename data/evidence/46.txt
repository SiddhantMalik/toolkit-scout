It s built on the Model Context Protocol , an open standard for connecting AI applications to outside tools, so assistants like Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT can look things up and make changes on Squarespace in the middle of a conversation. With it, someone can describe a new business and leave the chat with a domain ready to buy, or ask which orders still need shipping? about their own store and get a live answer. Connect a Client â Add Squarespace to Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, or another MCP client. Notice: If you are a Squarespace user who is accessing the Squarespace MCP server through an AI assistant, for example to search for a domain or manage your own website, your use is governed by our Terms of Service . If you re a developer building an experience for your users and connecting your own app or agent directly to the Squarespace MCP server, for example to add domain search to your product, your use is governed by our Developer Terms of Use . What you can do The server s tools are organized into groups. Each group has its own page describing what its tools do, what to ask for, and what to watch out for. Group What it s for Sign-in Domains Brainstorm business names, check domain availability and pricing, and get a link to buy a domain on Squarespace. Not required Commerce Find, create, and update contacts and products, and look up orders, on one of your websites. Currently only available in Claude and ChatGPT. Required How it works The Squarespace MCP server is a remote server. There s nothing to install. Your MCP client connects directly to: Code https://mcp.squarespace.com/mcp You don t call the tools yourself. You ask the assistant for something in plain language, and it decides which tools to call and in what order. The tool descriptions the assistant needs are sent by the server when it connects, so they don t need to be copied into your prompts. Signing in The Domains tools work for anyone, with no Squarespace account required. The Commerce tools read and change a website, so they need you to sign in with your Squarespace account. They only appear in your client once you ve signed in, and they act with your own permissions: an assistant can only see and change what you could see and change yourself in that website s Commerce panel. See Connect a Client . Working with a website Every signed-in tool works on one website at a time. If it isn t clear which site you mean, the assistant will ask, then keep using that site for the rest of the conversation until you ask to switch. What the tools can and can t change Domains tools never buy anything. They return a checkout link. A domain is only registered when someone opens that link and completes checkout on squarespace.com. Commerce tools don t move money. They can create and update contacts and products, but they can t take payments, issue refunds, or fulfill orders. Orders are read-only. Changes are real. A product or contact the assistant creates or edits is saved to your live website. New products are hidden from shoppers until you (or the assistant, if you ask) publish them. Most MCP clients ask you to approve a tool before it makes a change. Keep that approval step turned on for tools that write to your website. Supported clients Squarespace is built into Gemini as a connected app, and the Domains tools also work in Claude, ChatGPT, and any client that supports remote MCP servers. The Commerce tools are currently only available in Claude and ChatGPT. See Connect a Client for setup steps. Resellers handle discovery, purchase, subscription management and billing, while Squarespace provisions and hosts the products. Products Available for Resale Resellers can resell one or more of these Squarespace products: 1) Squarespace Domains Squarespace offers domain names across 400+ Top-Level Domains (TLDs) -- e.g. .COM, .NET, .ORG, .BIZ, .SITE, .AI. Via the Reseller API, resellers can enable customers to search for, select and purchase a custom domain (e.g. www.mycustomdomain.com ) within the reseller s own UX. Customers can then manage their purchased domain on Squarespace -- e.g. configure nameservers and DNS settings, and manage WHOIS privacy. Some of these actions are also possible via the Reseller API, should a reseller choose to facilitate them from their environment. 2) Squarespace Websites Squarespace helps users build beautiful, professional websites that perfectly represents their ideas, brands, or businesses. Via the Reseller API, resellers can enable customers to purchase a Squarespace website within the reseller s own UX. Customers can then build their website within a few clicks on Squarespace using our Blueprint AI tool, and continue enhancing their site with Squarespace tools and integrations. All Squarespace products are subscription-based. See Subscription Model to understand important subscription concepts. Pricing and Packaging There are two aspects to pricing: 1) what Squarespace charges the reseller 2) what the reseller charges end-customers. 1) Pricing between Squarespace and the reseller Squarespace charges resellers for each subscription resold, based on a wholesale rate discounted from Squarespace retail. Resellers receive an invoice 3-5 business days after the end of each month. Resold domains are priced by TLD, per year (e.g. all .COM domains have the same annual price, as do all .APP domains, .CO domains, .BIO domains, etc.) . Domains are available as annual or multi-year subscriptions. For multi-year terms, the annual price at time purchase is applied to all years of the purchased term. Resold websites are priced by plan (Core, Advanced, Plus), per month or per year -- i.e. there are six total price points, as each plan can be offered as a monthly or annual subscription. Please see full reseller pricing details here . Pricing will not change often, and we will notify resellers at least 60 days in advance of any changes. 2) End-Customer Pricing Packaging Resellers are free to set pricing for end-customers however they wish â e.g. pass on their wholesale rate, charge a premium, bundle resold products, offer discounts for bulk purchases, etc. Resellers are also free to select which specific products they offer. For example they can exclude TLDs that have extra data gathering requirements; or solely offer the most popular Squarespace website subscription, which is the Core tier on an annual term. Refunds Refunds from Squarespace to the reseller are available for new website subscriptions within 14 days of purchase. Domains are not eligible for refunds, nor website renewals. Refunds to end-customers are at the discretion of the reseller. Refunds are requested via the existing subscription cancellation endpoint . For cancellation requests where isRefund:true â If eligibility criteria are met, Squarespace will cancel the subscription per existing processes and refund the charge in your next monthly invoice. Refunds requested for ineligible subscriptions will return a 400 with a corresponding error message. Note that refundReason is currently for internal Squarespace logging only. Server URL Code https://mcp.squarespace.com/mcp Use this URL in Claude, ChatGPT, and any other MCP client. Menu names in each client change between releases, so the steps below may not match your version exactly. Claude Open Settings â Connectors and choose to add a custom connector. Enter https://mcp.squarespace.com/mcp as the server URL. To use the Commerce tools, connect and sign in to Squarespace when prompted. Start a new chat with the connector turned on. Claude Code Add the server from your terminal: Code claude mcp add --transport http squarespace https://mcp.squarespace.com/mcp Then run /mcp in Claude Code and choose the Squarespace server to sign in. ChatGPT Open Settings â Apps Connectors (or Connectors , depending on your plan and version). Add a custom connector with the URL https://mcp.squarespace.com/mcp . Sign in to Squarespace when prompted if you want to use the Commerce tools. Turn the connector on for a new chat. Any other MCP client Configure a remote MCP server with: URL: https://mcp.squarespace.com/mcp Transport: Streamable HTTP Only the Domains tools are available in other clients. The Commerce tools are currently only available in Claude and ChatGPT. Sign in to Squarespace The Domains tools work without an account. The Commerce tools need you to sign in with the Squarespace account you use to manage your websites, and are currently only available in Claude and ChatGPT. When your client offers to connect or sign in, you re taken to the standard Squarespace login page. After you sign in, your client receives access to the signed-in tools and they appear in its tool list. Until then, only the Domains tools are listed. Signing in doesn t give the assistant more access than you have. Every tool acts as you, with the permissions you have on the website it s working on. To disconnect, remove or sign out of the connector in your client s settings. For client developers: sign-in uses OAuth 2.1. The authorization server is advertised in the server s protected resource metadata at https://mcp.squarespace.com/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/mcp . Verify your connection After connecting, try: Is example-test-domain.com available on Squarespace? The assistant should check the domain and reply with availability and pricing. If you ve signed in, also try: Show me my five most recent orders. The assistant should reply with orders from your store. If it says it has no Squarespace tools for this, check that you completed sign-in and that the connector is turned on for the chat.
