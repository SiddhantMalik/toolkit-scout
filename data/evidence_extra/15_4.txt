access for Pylon support — — ✓ Frequently asked questions How is Pylon priced? Pylon is priced based on the number of seats and the number of agentic credits your team needs. Because B2B support teams vary significantly in size and workflow, pricing reflects your team's unique needs. Book a demo and we'll put together a quote specific to your setup. Is pricing per seat, per ticket, or per account? Pylon's pricing is seat-based and credit usage-based. You're not charged per ticket, so your costs stay predictable as your customer base grows, even as ticket volume scales. What's included in every plan? Every plan includes core ticketing, unified ticketing (Slack, email, and chat widget), omnichannel support, a knowledge base, NPS and CSAT surveys, and analytics. Are AI features included, or are they a separate add-on? Pylon's agentic features (including the Assist Agent, Automations, Support Agent, and Slack Agent) are accessible when you purchase credits, not sold as separate add-ons. Does Pylon offer annual and monthly billing, and is there a discount for paying annually? Pylon offers both annual and monthly billing. When you commit to credits upfront, you will receive a discount. Do I have to buy credits upfront? No. Pylon offers both pay-as-you-go credits and committed credit plans, so teams can buy credits as they need them or commit upfront. You can purchase additional credits in-product anytime. Is there a minimum seat requirement or minimum contract size? There is a minimum seat count for new customers. Pylon is built for B2B support teams rather than solo operators, so it's designed to deliver value at team scale. What does a contract with Pylon look like? Standard contracts are annual. Multi-year agreements are available for teams that want rate lock and additional flexibility. Credits can be used across the entire contract term and buying more at once will result in a higher credit discount. What counts as a "seat"? A seat covers any teammate who needs to work tickets, including agents and admins with inbox access. Stakeholders who only need visibility into conversations, without working them, can use a viewer seat. Do you have viewer seats? Yes, Pylon has view-only seats. Your sales representative will work with you on the number of viewer seats your team needs. Does Pylon offer a free trial or free plan? Pylon offers free trials for prospects. Your sales representative will work with you on setting up the demo organization. Is there a minimum number of accounts required for Account Intelligence? Yes. Account Intelligence requires a minimum of 50 customer accounts. The platform that fits exactly what your team needs. Join 1,800+ B2B companies who have left legacy platforms behind. Thank you!
