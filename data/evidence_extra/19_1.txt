--- updatedAt: 2025-09-16T01:31:26.000Z agentTools: projectIndex: https://developers.gorgias.com/llms.txt --- # Private vs. Public App Understand the difference between the two types and decide which one better fits your goals. Before you start creating your first Gorgias app you need to choose between two types of apps: ## 🔐 Private apps These apps should be used if you plan to access only your own Gorgias account data. * You don't need to follow any special process to create a private app. * For the authentication method, you can use [Access Tokens](https://developers.gorgias.com/docs/access-tokens-api-keys). ## 👥 Public apps These apps are published in our App Store and are available to thousands of shop owners. * You need to follow a [specific process](https://developers.gorgias.com/docs/overview) which includes a review from the Gorgias team. <HTMLBlock>{` <blockquote class="callout callout_warn" theme="🚧"> <h3 class="callout-heading empty"><span class="callout-icon">👉</span></h3> <p>Public app authentication can be done only via <strong>OAuth2</strong> for which you will need to register on our <a target="_blank" href="https://partners.gorgias.com/signup">Developer portal</a>.</p></blockquote> `}</HTMLBlock>
