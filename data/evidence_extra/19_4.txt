--- updatedAt: 2025-09-16T01:46:05.000Z agentTools: projectIndex: https://developers.gorgias.com/llms.txt --- # 5. Submit app for review Congratulations! You've built your app and it will soon become available to thousands of Gorgias customers.\ There are only a couple of final steps that separate you from this moment. * You need to get back to your [Draft App form](https://developers.gorgias.com/docs/2-create-a-draft-app-submission-form) and complete all the fields that you haven't completed yet. If you're unsure about some fields and not ready to submit your app yet, you can always click on **Save** and come back to this page later. * Add screenshots ([guide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1qj-KRqAatZ5m7WTUKf7LLRWvcm2836Ud_wzCxElm0S4/edit?usp=sharing)) to the app form, they will appear on the listing and make your app more compelling for merchants. * Once done, click on **Submit Application**. * Our tech partnership team will review your app to make sure it works properly and is aligned with our expectations. * Once we complete the review we will reach out to you to let you know about the result. If it is approved, it will appear in both the in-product App Store and the [public App Store page](https://www.gorgias.com/apps). If it is not approved, we will let you know what needs to be fixed/changed for your app to be approved.
