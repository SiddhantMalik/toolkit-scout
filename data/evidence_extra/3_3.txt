Pipedrive MCP (Model Context Protocol) connects your AI assistant directly to Pipedrive, allowing it to securely access, update and act on your CRM data using natural language. What can I do with it? You can query CRM data, update records and automate workflows directly from your AI assistant. Find deals, update contacts, create activities, analyze pipelines and automate repetitive tasks using natural “plain” language. Who can use the Pipedrive MCP? All Pipedrive customers, on every plan. Usage is subject to token limits based on your plan and you can purchase additional tokens if needed. How do I get started with the Pipedrive MCP server? Connect your AI assistant to Pipedrive in minutes through secure OAuth, no coding or developer required. See the Pipedrive MCP documentation for step-by-step instructions. Which AI assistants are supported? Pipedrive MCP works with any AI assistant that supports custom MCP connections. We provide setup guides for ChatGPT and Claude and other assistants can connect using the Pipedrive MCP server URL. Does it adhere to my existing Pipedrive permissions? Yes. Your AI assistant can only access and modify data your Pipedrive user already has permission to view and edit. All actions are recorded in the change log for full auditability. Is my data safe? Yes. Pipedrive MCP is built and maintained by Pipedrive and follows our security standards. Your AI assistant can only access data within the scope of your permissions. Your AI provider's own privacy and security policies also apply. Is there an extra cost? Pipedrive MCP is available on all Pipedrive plans.
