Log in Try it free Newsroom Press releases Sales insight reports Press kit Press contacts In the news Pipedrive launches native MCP server, bringing CRM workflows directly into AI assistants New York, June 30, 2026 – Pipedrive , the easy and intelligent CRM for small and medium-sized businesses, today announced the launch of its native Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, enabling customers to securely connect Pipedrive with AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and other MCP-compatible tools. MCP is an open standard that enables AI assistants to securely connect to external tools and data sources. Through Pipedrive’s native MCP server, AI assistants can interact with live CRM data in real time while respecting existing user permissions and maintaining a full audit trail of actions performed. Using the Pipedrive MCP server, customers can: Search deals, contacts, organizations and leads using natural language Create and update CRM records without manual data entry Convert leads into deals and manage activities Generate pipeline insights and sales analysis Turn meeting notes into structured CRM records Automate follow-up actions and multi-step sales workflows Execute other intelligent automations and continuously evolving AI capabilities The launch allows sales professionals to access, update and act on CRM data directly from their AI assistant using natural language. Instead of switching between applications, users can search deals, create records, update contacts, schedule activities, analyze pipelines and automate sales workflows from within their preferred AI interface. “Salespeople increasingly start their day with AI assistants. The question is no longer whether CRM data should be available in those environments, but how to make that access secure, reliable and genuinely useful,” said Joe Futty, Chief Product & Technology Officer at Pipedrive. “With Pipedrive MCP, we’re giving customers direct, secure access to their sales data and workflows wherever work happens. This is an important step toward a future where Pipedrive's intelligence isn’t confined to a single interface, it’s embedded in every tool, every conversation and every decision a sales team makes." “We used Pipedrive MCP to bring AI directly into the CRM, handling the manual work that used to slow salespeople down: data enrichment, lead discovery, analysis, reminders, automations and connecting third-party tools. All without leaving Pipedrive. This allows salespeople to focus on what AI still can’t replace: the human side of sales, like reading between the lines, building trust and closing deals through genuine relationships,” said Bernd Auer, Pipedrive consultant at PD Experts. “MCP will fundamentally shift the relationship between AI and CRM. Instead of AI summarizing data after the fact, it becomes an active participant in the sales process. Pipedrive becomes a trusted data backbone that makes AI actually useful in practice.” Unlike custom API integrations or unofficial third-party connectors, Pipedrive MCP is built and maintained by Pipedrive, requires no coding, and works with real-time CRM data. Every action performed through an AI assistant follows the user’s existing permissions and is recorded in Pipedrive’s change logs for full transparency and accountability. The launch reflects a broader shift in how professionals interact with software. As AI assistants become increasingly central to daily work, businesses are looking for ways to bring trusted business context into those environments without sacrificing security, reliability or control. Pipedrive MCP is available to customers on all Pipedrive plans. The server works with any AI assistant that supports custom MCP connections, including ChatGPT and Claude, with setup completed through a secure OAuth connection and no developer resources required.
