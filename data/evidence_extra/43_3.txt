The Marketplace team reviews every app submission and tests the app against the standards on this page before listing it in the App Marketplace . Check your app against each section before you submit it for approval . Some sections apply only to apps with certain features, such as storefront scripts, orders, or Unified Billing. Each of those sections says who it applies to. The Marketplace team reviews app submissions and isn’t a support team, so it may take time to respond to other requests. For questions that aren’t about your app submission, contact BigCommerce support . How to read the requirements Every requirement has a level: Level Meaning Required The Marketplace team checks this on every submission it applies to. Your app must meet it to be approved. Recommended Doesn’t block approval, but we strongly encourage it. Each recommendation explains the benefit to store owners or to your app. Before you submit Requirement Level Your Technology Partner account is valid and active. To create one, see Becoming a partner . Required The primary contact on your partner account has accepted the Technology Partner Agreement. Required A contact on your partner account has the Billing/Financial role. Required Your Partner ID is entered in the Developer Portal. Required Listing All listing fields are required, except Videos , Case Studies , and the Alternate logo . Listings should be well worded, cleanly formatted, and follow the wording and image specifications below. For a walkthrough of each field in the Developer Portal, see Publishing apps . Name and branding Requirement Level The app name is your brand or product name, and matches the name used in your logos, descriptions, and the app itself. Required The app name contains no taglines, keywords, or promotional terms, such as “Best”, “Free”, “#1”, or “Now with AI”. Put descriptors in the app summary instead. Required The app name and logos don’t include BigCommerce or Commerce branding. Required References to BigCommerce elsewhere in the listing follow the branding guidelines in the Media Kit . Required The listing and the app’s dashboard don’t reference competing commerce platforms, such as Shopify, WooCommerce, or Adobe Commerce (Magento). Mentioning Salesforce on its own is fine. Required Support Requirement Level The support website URL loads a live page that includes contact information, such as an email address, form, phone number, or chat. Required The support email is a valid business address. Personal or free-domain addresses, such as Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, or iCloud, aren’t accepted. Required Support resources are available throughout the app, including during onboarding and on the app’s dashboard inside the BigCommerce iframe. Required Summary, description, and categories Requirement Level The app summary accurately describes the app and is 128 characters or fewer. Required The app description accurately describes the app’s functionality and value. Required The selected categories accurately reflect what the app does. Required Images and video Requirement Level The app icon is 200×200 px, or square at an equivalent resolution, and matches the app’s official branding. Required The app icon stays legible at 32 px, so it’s recognizable in the control panel Apps menu. Recommended The primary logo is 350×130 px, or matches that aspect ratio. It shows only your brand logo on a white background. Required The alternate logo is optional. If you provide one, it is 259×158 px or matches that aspect ratio, and shows only your brand logo on a color background. Required if provided Screenshots are the same size and in widescreen (landscape) format. They show demo or placeholder data, with no real personal data and no competitor or merchant branding. Required Videos are optional. If you provide one, enter the video ID, not the full URL. Required if provided Features and case studies Requirement Level The app lists no more than five features. Titles are brief, and descriptions are clear and free of stray markdown or HTML. Required Case studies are optional. If you provide them, include no more than four. Links must work, and each title is brief and descriptive. Required if provided Each case study summary is a single paragraph, with no bullets, headings, or line breaks. Required if provided Each case study features a BigCommerce or Commerce merchant, or is platform-agnostic. A case study that names a competing platform in the merchant’s tech stack fails review. Required if provided Legal pages and guides Requirement Level The privacy policy and terms of service URLs load real policy pages. A homepage or placeholder page doesn’t qualify. Required At least one guide, either an installation guide or a user guide, is live and reachable. Required Guides contain real integration documentation. A homepage or generic marketing page doesn’t qualify. Required Pricing Requirement Level Pricing details are accurate. Select Free to use only if a genuine free plan exists. If the app is free to install but requires a paid subscription to use, list the starting price. Required The billing type matches your model: Free to use , Monthly fee , Upfront cost only , or Upfront cost + monthly fee . Use Other only for a non-standard model. For example, entering “$5/month” in Other fails review. Required Installation Requirement Level The app is single-click and uses the OAuth flow to authenticate. Required The install completes without errors, and the app appears in My Apps afterward. Required The app requests only the OAuth scopes its functionality needs. Apps configured with all scopes aren’t approved. Set Checkout Content to Modify only if the app modifies checkout (see Security and checkout ). For a list of scopes, see OAuth scopes . Required The app serves all callback URLs over HTTPS. Required The app is multi-user enabled . Required The app doesn’t store bearer tokens against the user’s email address, which avoids problems with ownership changes and multiple users. Required The app responds to the install callback with styled and branded HTML for the control panel iframe. The page can’t be blank or broken, and it follows the user-interface constraints . Required A store owner can complete setup and onboarding from start to finish. Required If the app’s landing page links to an external dashboard, the link opens in a new tab or window. The dashboard doesn’t load in place of the BigCommerce control panel frame. Required The app includes onboarding instructions and lists any setup requirements after installation, so store owners can start using the app without searching for next steps. Recommended Install and authorization stay inside the BigCommerce control panel, without redirecting to an external site, so store owners aren’t interrupted mid-install. Recommended The app offers registration for both new and existing users, so store owners with an existing account on your platform can connect it. Recommended The app autofills registration fields, such as email address and store URL, from the store’s information, which shortens sign-up. Recommended The app doesn’t ask store owners for information it can already get, such as the store’s email address and domain. Retrieve it from the auth callback or the Store Information API . Recommended The app UI uses BigDesign , so it matches the look of the control panel. Recommended Functionality Requirement Level The app works as advertised, from start to finish, and doesn’t conflict with BigCommerce functionality. Required The app is production ready. The app and its callback URLs run in a production environment. Staging and sandbox apps aren’t listed in the App Marketplace. Required Data the app exchanges with BigCommerce through the APIs is accurate. Required The app never passes sensitive data in query parameters, where it can end up in logs and browser history. Required The app uses BigCommerce APIs wherever possible. For example, don’t require a store owner to import a product CSV manually if the app can retrieve those products using the API. Required The app uses current, supported API versions and no deprecated endpoints. It uses V3 endpoints in favor of V2 when feature parity exists. For details, see Deprecations and sunsets . Required API requests from the app are stable, with normal error rates. Required The app’s client-side code works in the BigCommerce supported browsers , including Chrome, Firefox, and Safari. Required The submission’s test instructions include everything the Marketplace team needs to test the app, including any test credentials for the app and for third-party services it requires. Required Apps that add another marketplace or sales channel to a store use the Channels Toolkit and follow the channel app requirements . Required The app doesn’t rely on third-party cookies and uses JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) to pass information. The app loads in an iframe, and Safari and other browsers already block third-party cookies by default. Chrome users can block them too. Recommended Storefront scripts Applies to apps that inject scripts into the storefront or customize themes. Requirement Level Scripts are created with the Scripts API , which adds them to Script Manager, and are not hardcoded into themes. For details, see Scripts API . Required Scripts are scoped to the pages where they’re needed, not applied globally when page-specific scoping fits. Required Each script has an appropriate consent_category : essential , functional , analytics , or targeting . Use essential only for core transactional functionality. Required Scripts and storefront components render and work on Stencil storefronts and across supported browsers. Required The app doesn’t conflict with native BigCommerce storefront features. Theme customizations don’t break other storefront functionality. Required The app customizes the storefront with the most efficient method available: the Scripts API first, then the Widgets API , and direct theme file edits only as a last resort. If theme edits are unavoidable, document them clearly. This keeps themes upgradeable for store owners. Recommended Orders Applies to apps that create orders. Requirement Level Orders include an accurate payment_method value and other accurate order details. Required Orders include the external_source field in the request body, with your app’s ID as the value. Required Unified Billing Applies to apps that charge store owners through Unified Billing . Requirement Level Selecting a plan redirects to the Unified Billing checkout. Required Checkout completes successfully, and Return to app redirects to the app. Required The app reflects an active subscription after checkout. Required The app lets store owners cancel an active subscription. Required Security and checkout Requirement Level The Checkout Content scope is set to Modify only if the app needs to modify checkout. If it doesn’t, disable the scope before submitting. Required Apps that access the Checkout Content scope are subject to a security assessment by BigCommerce’s security team. Required Apps that modify the checkout experience use the BigCommerce Checkout SDK . Required Apps that process transactions or handle credit card data must pass a PCI Compliance review by BigCommerce’s security team and provide both a PCI-DSS Attestation of Compliance and a data flow diagram. BigCommerce only accepts pre-approved new payment gateway app submissions for App Marketplace review. Required We have paused all new externally built payment integrations for our publicly available BigCommerce Marketplace. We are updating our available APIs to create a more robust experience and hope to re-open this program in the near future. Uninstall Requirement Level After the app is uninstalled, the control panel and the storefront return to their pre-installation state. Required Storefront and checkout scripts are removed automatically on uninstall. Required Compatibility badges In the Developer Portal, you can declare that your app supports Multi-Storefront (MSF), Catalyst, or Multi-Location Inventory (MLI). Declaring support requests a compatibility review. The Marketplace team adds the matching badge to your listing after your app passes it. For the Developer Portal fields, see Publishing apps . Multi-Storefront Applies to apps that declare MSF support. For guidance, see Optimizing multi-storefront apps . Requirement Level The app works correctly across multiple storefront channels. Required Storefront content the app creates is inserted on, and works on, all BigCommerce storefronts. Required URLs and domain references the app generates match the correct storefront domain. Required Catalyst Applies to apps that declare Catalyst support. For guidance, see Building Catalyst-compatible apps and integrations . Requirement Level The app is compatible with Multi-Storefront and supports MSF use cases on Catalyst. Required The app recognizes Catalyst as an independent storefront channel and handles MSF data interactions correctly for Catalyst storefronts. Required Storefront content appears and works correctly on Catalyst. Required If the app can’t deliver its storefront functionality through the Scripts API alone, it provides clear guidance for Catalyst theme edits. Required Multi-Location Inventory Applies to apps that declare MLI support and interact with catalog inventory. Requirement Level The app uses the V3 Inventory API, not legacy inventory endpoints. Using the V2 stock field fails review. Required If your app can’t meet these requirements, email appstore@bigcommerce.com to discuss your options for approval. FAQ Are all fields required? For App Marketplace approval, you’ll need to fill out all fields on your listing with applicable content and links. These will be reviewed as part of the Marketplace approval process. Case Studies , Videos , and the Alternate logo are optional.
