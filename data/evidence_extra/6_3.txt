Offers may vary by partner or over time. Need Help? We offer custom plans & pricing for larger teams. Talk to an Expert FAQs Can I Have a Private Workspace? Yes! While the Podio platform is a collaborative tool, sometimes work is confidential and should not be seen by everyone in an organization. Private workspaces are invite-only—users that are not members of the workspace do not have access. How Many Items Can I Create? You can create unlimited items in any of the paid plans. On the free plan, you have a limit of 100 items per organization. The total number of items created across all your apps and workspaces in the organization cannot exceed 100 on the free plan. Learn more about the Item Limit. Not sure what an item, app or workspace is? Find out more . What Are Automated Workflows? You can use simple workflows to automate your processes in the Podio platform. Workflows are triggered using a "when/then" format. For example, you can set up a workflow that functions like this: when you change the status of an item to "complete," then a task is automatically assigned to the person who needs to review the item. Find out more . What Is Advanced Workflow Automation? Advanced Workflow Automation gives Premium organizations access to workflow capabilities designed for complex and scalable business processes. It helps extend automation beyond the Podio platform through third-party integrations and advanced actions such as sending emails and generating PDFs. Find out more . Can I Try Podio Premium Before Upgrading? Yes. Eligible free plan organizations can start a 30-day Podio Premium Trial directly inside the platform by clicking “Try Premium.” Trial-specific limits apply. Find out more . How Is an Annual Plan Different From a Month-to-Month Plan? With an annual plan, you pay for one year up front and receive a discount. Your plan will automatically renew at the next annual billing cycle. Additionally, on a Premium annual plan, you have access to Podio advanced integrations, like extension vouchers and sales dashboards from our partners. What Is the Difference Between an Employee and a Client? Employees and clients in Podio software are differentiated by their permission types. Employees are paid users with advanced workspace permissions, including app and workflow management, while clients—such as contractors and freelancers—are free users with limited permissions like viewing, commenting, and uploading files. Learn more about permissions here . What Is User Management? User management lets administrators oversee and control all members in the organization. You can view members, remove members, assign admin roles, and convert clients to employees or vice versa. Find out more . What Is the Light User Role? Podio software lets you collaborate with external users, such as clients, partners, and freelancers. The light role provides limited permissions for these users. Light users can't edit, add, or delete apps, nor can they invite people to your workspaces. Find out more . What if I Need Custom Integrations? We have partners available worldwide to assist with custom integrations, implementation and training for your team. Find a partner here . You can also enhance Podio capabilities with our partner built extensions and open API .
