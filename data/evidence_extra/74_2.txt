Supported clients Atlassian MCP supports several clients, including: OpenAI ChatGPT Claude Docker Google Gemini Amazon Quick Suite Depending on the client you're using, the setup process may vary. We recommending checking the setup procedure of your client of choice, or even use the client’s AI capabilities, to set up Atlassian MCP. Disclaimer MCP clients can perform actions across Atlassian apps with your existing permissions. Use least privilege, review high‑impact changes before confirming, and monitor audit logs for unusual activity. Learn more: MCP Clients - Understanding the potential security risks Need help?
