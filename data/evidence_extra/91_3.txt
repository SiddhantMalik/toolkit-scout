FAQs about Gemini Notebook Enterprise

This page provides answers to frequently asked questions (FAQs) about
Gemini Notebook Enterprise.
Can I move my notebooks to a different organization?
No, you can't directly transfer or share notebooks between different
Google Cloud organizations. To move your data, you need to manually export your
notebooks and sources from one organization and then import them into an account
in the other organization. For more information, see
Share notebooks
.
Can I use my Gemini Notebook Plus notebooks with Gemini Notebook Enterprise?
No, Gemini Notebook Plus is designed for Google Workspace and isn't a
Google Cloud product. Your existing Gemini Notebook Plus notebooks can't be
used or directly accessed in Gemini Notebook Enterprise or Gemini Enterprise.
Can I enable Nano Banana in Gemini Notebook Enterprise ?
No, Nano Banana isn't supported in Gemini Notebook Enterprise.
Does Model Armor support data masking or de-identification in Gemini Notebook Enterprise?
No. Model Armor does not support data de-identification or masking
in Gemini Notebook Enterprise. If a prompt or response triggers a
configured Sensitive Data Protection detector and the enforcement type is
Inspect and block
, Gemini Notebook Enterprise blocks the request or
response rather than de-identifying it. For more information, see
Considerations when using Model Armor
.
Why can't I add a website URL as a source in Gemini Notebook Enterprise?
If your project is protected by
VPC Service Controls
, adding public or external
website URLs as notebook sources is disabled.
Adding a website URL as a source performs a live crawl of the public web,
which generates outbound network traffic outside Google networks. Because
VPC Service Controls blocks outbound network traffic to maintain security and
compliance, direct website ingestion is blocked for VPC Service Controls-protected
projects.
Other source types, such as Google Drive documents and YouTube video links,
remain supported. YouTube video links remain supported because YouTube
transcripts are retrieved using an internal service rather than live public web
crawling.
