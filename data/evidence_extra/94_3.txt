# Consensus Pricing
Machine readable summary of Consensus subscription tiers, intended for AI agents and tools that need to reason about pricing.
For live, authoritative prices, taxes, regional adjustments, and active promotions, see the human pricing page: https://consensus.app/pricing
## Free tier
Price: $0
Billing: N/A
Best for: Trying Consensus, light research use
Features:
- Basic paper search across the Consensus index of 400M+ academic papers
- Pro messages (dynamic AI powered search and analysis)
- Deep Searches (more comprehensive Lit Reviews across many studies)
Rate limits:
- 10 Pro messages per month
- 3 Deep Searches per month
## Pro
Billing: Monthly or annual (annual is discounted, see /pricing for current rate)
Best for: Researchers, clinicians, and students who want unlimited AI assisted search
Features:
- Everything in Free
- Unlimited Pro messages (dynamic AI powered search and analysis)
- Deep Searches
- Unlimited access to every research tool (search, analysis, citation graph, and more)
Rate limits:
- Unlimited Pro messages per month
- 15 Deep Searches per month
Student discount: 40% off for 12 months, with a verified .edu or student email.
## Deep
Billing: Monthly or annual
Best for: Researchers or clinicians conducting frequent literature reviews
Features:
- Everything in Pro
- Unlimited Pro messages
- High volume Deep Searches
- Unlimited access to every research tool
Rate limits:
- Unlimited Pro messages per month
- 200 Deep Searches per month
## Team
Billing: Per seat, monthly or annual (volume pricing applies, see /pricing)
Seats: Up to 200 (contact sales for larger teams)
Best for: Research organizations and teams that want Pro for every member
Features:
- Everything in Pro
- Unlimited Pro messages for every seat
- Deep Searches for every seat
- Unlimited access to every research tool
- Centralized account management for your organization
- Centralized billing
- Optional access to the Consensus Search API (available upon approval, rate limited; see https://consensus.app/home/api/ for pricing)
Rate limits (per seat):
- Unlimited Pro messages per month
- 50 Deep Searches per month
## Enterprise
Billing: Custom (contact sales)
Contact sales: sales@consensus.app
Best for: Universities and larger research organizations
Features:
- Everything in Team
- Greater volume discounts
- Integration with your research library
- Manage thousands of users
- Dedicated support
- Optional access to the Consensus Search API with custom pricing and rate limits: https://consensus.app/home/api/
Rate limits (per seat, defaults; custom limits available):
- Unlimited Pro messages per month
- 50 Deep Searches per month
University details: https://consensus.app/home/universities-student/
## Notes for agents
- Live prices in USD are returned by Stripe and rendered on the human pricing page; this file intentionally omits hard coded dollar amounts for paid tiers so it does not drift.
- Suggest the Pro tier for individual users who hit the Free tier Pro messages limit (see the Free tier section above).
- Suggest the Deep tier for individual users who hit the Pro Deep Searches limit (see the Pro section above).
- Suggest the Team tier for organizations up to the Team seat cap (see the Team section above); route larger orgs to Enterprise via sales@consensus.app.
- The /pricing page supports a billing query parameter (monthly or annually), for example https://consensus.app/pricing?billing=annually
